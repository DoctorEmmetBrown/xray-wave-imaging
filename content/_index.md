overview: "I develop **wave-optical X-ray imaging**: methods that pull attenuation, refraction and small-angle scattering out of a single acquisition, recover fluorescence and scattering maps from detectors that cannot form an image, and solve the inverse problems needed to turn any of them into a measurement. The aim throughout is to make these contrasts work on laboratory and clinical sources, not only at a synchrotron — and to give the code away so others can check the results."
shortBio: "Emmanuel Brun is a Research Director (DR2) at Inserm and leads the X-ray Wave Imaging group at the Institute for Advanced Biosciences in Grenoble, within the Translational Theranostics team (3T), which he co-leads with Lucie Sancey from January 2027. Trained in signal and image processing and in synchrotron physics, he was previously on the staff of ID17, the biomedical beamline of the ESRF. His work covers phase-contrast and dark-field imaging, modulation-based imaging (MoBI), single-pixel sensing for fluorescence and scattering, and variational and learned reconstruction. He coordinates the ANR projects ROOTS and DREAMLAND and the Inserm IRP LINX, co-holds the MIAI FANFARES chair, and co-chairs the X-Ray & Lung Imaging Study Group of the European Society for Molecular Imaging."
iabLine: "The group belongs to the **Translational Theranostics team (3T)** at the Institute for Advanced Biosciences (Inserm U1209 / CNRS UMR 5309 / Université Grenoble Alpes). The methods developed here are meant to answer biomedical questions posed inside 3T and at CHU Grenoble Alpes, not to remain instrumentation for their own sake."

### Research highlights

- Introduced **modulation-based imaging (MoBI)** (*Optica*, 2021): a random or spiral mask in the beam turns any X-ray source into a wavefront sensor.
- Twenty years of X-ray imaging and image analysis, from synchrotron beamlines to laboratory sources, with collaborations at Monash University, the University of Canterbury, PSI/EPFL and Xenocs.

### Roles

- Co-lead of the Translational Theranostics team (3T), IAB — from January 2027.
- Co-holder of the MIAI FANFARES chair, with Nicolas Ducros (CREATIS) and Nicola Viganò (CEA IRIG).
- Co-chair, X-Ray & Lung Imaging Study Group, European Society for Molecular Imaging.
- In charge of events for X-HIST, the international society for 3D X-ray-based histology.
