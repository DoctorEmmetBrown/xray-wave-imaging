## Objective

LINX formalises a long-standing collaboration with the groups that developed much of speckle- and modulation-based X-ray imaging. Its purpose is exchange rather than a single deliverable: extended visits in both directions, shared beamtime, joint students, and a common vocabulary for methods that are currently described five different ways in five different papers.

## Workshop 2027

An international workshop on phase imaging by modulation — at every wavelength, not only X-rays —
will take place in late May / early June 2027 at the CNRS Paul-Langevin centre in Aussois, in the
French Alps.
